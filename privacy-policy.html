Langur Burja Privacy Policy

App: Langur Burja (com.qrallrounder.langurburja) · Developer: Lampsoft Nepal · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Langur Burja game collects, why, and how you can delete it. Langur Burja uses virtual coins only. Coins cannot be bought and have no real-world value.

Information we collect

  • Account identifiers. When you play as a guest or sign in with Google Play Games, the game creates a player ID with Unity Authentication. If you use Google Play Games, we receive your Play Games player ID and display name.
  • Game data. Your coin balance, daily bonus and rewarded video history, and the rooms, bets and results of games you play online.
  • Information shown to other players. In online rooms, other players in the same room see your display name (your Play Games name, or "Guest" plus four characters), your bets and your results. There is no chat.
  • Advertising and device data. Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners. They may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, phone number or payment information.

How we use it

  • To run the game: keep your coin balance, run online rooms and settle bets fairly on our servers.
  • To keep your coins when you change phones (if you sign in with Google Play Games).
  • To show ads, including rewarded videos that give you coins, and to limit how often they appear.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse.

Service providers

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): sign-in, cloud save, server scripts and online rooms. Unity privacy policy
  • Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners: ads. Unity privacy policy
  • Google Play Games Services: optional sign-in. Google privacy policy

We do not sell your personal information. Data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • You can reset or limit ad personalization in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You can play without signing in to Google Play Games by choosing "Play as Guest".

Deleting your account and data

In the app: on the title screen, tap Delete account (bottom right) and confirm. This permanently deletes your player account, your coin balance and your saved game data.

Without the app: email lampsoftnepal@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Langur Burja account" and your display name. We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

Data held by advertising partners is handled under their own policies. We keep your data only while your account exists.

Children

Langur Burja contains simulated gambling with virtual coins and is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes and change the date above.
